# Typography and Styling Rules for Chakri Sheba

## 1. Font Family
- **SolaimanLipi Everywhere:** Only `SolaimanLipi` must be used across the entire website for all headings, body text, buttons, navigation, inputs, and badges. No other font may be used.

## 2. Button Font Size: 17px
- **All Buttons:** Every button (`<button>`, button-like `<a>` CTA links, submit buttons, filter/search action buttons) MUST have a font size of **17px** (`text-[17px]`).
- Do not make buttons small (`text-xs` or 12px/14px). Always maintain `17px` for buttons.

## 3. Paragraph & General Text Size: 16px
- **Normal Paragraphs and Text:** All general paragraphs (`<p>`), descriptions, list items, and standard body text must have a font size of **16px** (`text-[16px]` or `text-base`).
